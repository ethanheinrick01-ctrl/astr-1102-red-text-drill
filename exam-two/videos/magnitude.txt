Distance, dust, and the magnitude scale
Lecture 14 pp. 12–16
AI Voice Generator · Clear

Reverse the scale
On the magnitude scale, a smaller number means a brighter object. A difference of five magnitudes corresponds to a brightness factor of one hundred. Watch brightness fall as the magnitude number rises.

Use a common distance
Absolute magnitude means apparent magnitude at ten parsecs, with extinction removed. Moving the same star from ten to one hundred parsecs makes it one hundred times fainter and increases its apparent magnitude by five.

Add the dust
Dust also makes a star fainter. If you attribute all that dimming to distance, you overestimate the distance. Keep intrinsic luminosity, distance, and extinction separate when interpreting apparent magnitude.
