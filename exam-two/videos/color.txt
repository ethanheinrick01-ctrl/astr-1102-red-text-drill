Heat the photosphere
As surface temperature rises, the peak of a thermal spectrum shifts toward shorter wavelengths. A hotter star emits more blue light relative to visible light. Watch the spectral curve change, not just its label.

Compare the filters
The lecture compares the visual to blue brightness ratio: about one point eight seven for the Sun and zero point eight one for Bellatrix. The hotter star has the smaller visual to blue ratio.

Remove blue light with dust
Now keep the star hot but put dust in the line of sight. Dust preferentially removes blue light from the direct beam. A red observed color can mean a cooler star or a reddened hotter star; color alone does not resolve that ambiguity.
