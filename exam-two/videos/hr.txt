Read the axes
On this Hertzsprung Russell diagram, luminosity increases upward and surface temperature decreases toward the right. Moving to the right means cooler, not hotter. Use both coordinates to interpret a star.

Hold temperature fixed
At the same surface temperature, a more luminous star has a larger radius. Watch two stars share a temperature while their emitting areas and positions differ. Giants can be cool and still very luminous.

Hold luminosity fixed
At the same luminosity, a hotter star must be smaller. Hot but faint white dwarfs occupy the lower left. These comparisons show relationships, not the evolutionary track of one star; the main sequence is not a timeline from left to right.
