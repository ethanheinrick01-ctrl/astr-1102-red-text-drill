Regular signals need explanation
Jocelyn Bell recognized a regular radio pulse signal in nineteen sixty seven. The provisional Little Green Men nickname captured an early question, not a demonstrated origin. Discovering similar sources supported a natural astrophysical population. Regularity is evidence to explain; it does not by itself prove intelligent transmission.

A compact clock
The Crab pulsar repeats in about thirty three milliseconds. A rotating white dwarf could not remain bound at such a short period, but a much smaller neutron star can support rapid rotation. Period is time per cycle. Its reciprocal is frequency: a period of two hundredths of a second means fifty cycles each second.

Density and location
A neutron star packs roughly stellar mass into a city scale radius. At fixed mass, reducing radius by half makes the volume one eighth of its original value. The density therefore increases by a factor of eight. The Crab’s central pulsar and surrounding remnant support a connection between core collapse and a compact neutron star. This evidence does not make every explosion identical.
