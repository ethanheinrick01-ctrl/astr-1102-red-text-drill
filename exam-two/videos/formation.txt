Gather the core
Begin with a dense core inside a dark cloud. Gravity brings material inward. Bok globules are dusty star formation sites; a dark appearance does not mean nothing is happening inside.

Build a protostar and disk
The supplied sequence develops a central protostar, an envelope, and a rotating disk. Bipolar outflows follow the rotation axis. Herbig Haro features are associated with outflow activity, not a different name for the entire dark cloud.

Clear the surroundings
The sequence continues through a young star with a disk toward a stellar and planetary system. The envelope thins and the disk changes. This is a schematic path, not a guarantee that every core forms one star or follows the same ages and sizes.
