Neutrino evidence
Supernova nineteen eighty seven A occurred in the Large Magellanic Cloud. Its short neutrino burst directly supported the collapse energy channel. Only a tiny sample was detected: most particles missed Earth, and most crossing a detector passed through without interacting. Detected counts do not equal the total emitted.

An older ring lights up
The circumstellar ring was already present before the explosion. Ejecta and the outward shock traveled toward it. When the shock encountered dense knots, energy deposited in the gas caused brightening. The changing ring therefore records an interaction with the progenitor’s earlier surroundings, rather than newly formed ring material.

State the pathway
A Sun like star sheds a planetary nebula and leaves a white dwarf. Successful massive core collapse can leave a neutron star; continued collapse can lead to a black hole. Specify the pathway before choosing a remnant. A missing central detection, by itself, does not uniquely establish a black hole.
